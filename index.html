Questions

Do I need soil or a yard?

No. The roots sit in the tower, and the pump keeps water and nutrients moving past them. A corner of an apartment, a balcony or a bright room is enough.

How much space does it take?

About a 53 cm circle of floor and 165 cm of height.

How does shipping work?

Every order ships by FedEx, and shipping is included in the $505 price.

When are the workshops?

Four times a year in Edmonton. You choose your season at checkout.

Is this a real store?

Not yet. This page is a proof-of-concept built for our BUSN 201 presentation, so you can see what ordering would feel like. Nothing is charged and nothing ships.
